About

My research interests include agentic systems, multimodal reasoning, generative modeling, and Bayesian optimization. In particular, I am interested in the intersection of these methods with regard to applications within scientific and societal domains. Critical examples include automating and accelerating discovery campaigns (i.e., materials, medicines, etc.) and engineering design (i.e., model and simulation tuning, principled experiment design, etc.).
